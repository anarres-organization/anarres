# Code of conduct

Anarres is committed to a respectful, rigorous, and welcoming research community.

Contributors should critique ideas and evidence without attacking people, represent results honestly, respect confidentiality and authorship, and make space for collaborators with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, fabrication, retaliation, and deliberate misrepresentation are unacceptable. Concerns should be reported privately to the repository owners until a community moderation team is established.
