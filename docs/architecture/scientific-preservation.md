# Preserving domain science

The Anarres CLI makes domain studies inspectable and interoperable. It does not
replace their models, estimands, decision rules, or native evidence.

An environment profile is a typed projection with four responsibilities:

1. identify the environment, starting subject, source, and measurement contract
2. record the domain adapter and implementation revision that produced it
3. expose selected measurements with versioned definitions and units
4. link to native evidence and qualification records by URI and digest

The originating study remains authoritative. Reproducing a claim still requires
its native protocol, records, and analysis. The CLI can show that two candidates
were measured compatibly, but it cannot infer that a coordinate predicts utility
unless a separate validated decision rule establishes that relationship.

## Legal reasoning

The legal program contains two distinct scientific objects. The SylloGym study
uses readiness, eligible-response availability, reward discrimination, token
cost, truncation, reliability, missingness, and a coverage floor to construct a
mixture. The LAB study uses task and workflow structure to estimate regression
risk across held-out practice areas. Neither object is equivalent to grouped
reward mixedness or effective coverage.

An adapter can export selected measurements from either study, but it must use
different measurement contracts and coordinate definitions. The native frozen
decision, checkpoint record, regression endpoint, validation split, and
uncertainty analysis remain linked artifacts rather than flattened profile
attributes.

## Robotics

The robotics program keeps target relevance, policy weakness, coverage,
data-quality correction, reliability, missingness, exposure, cost, native
outcomes, and guardrails separate. It also distinguishes a correct abstention
from selector superiority and retains physical outcomes in their native units.

An adapter must preserve episode and task identity, policy and dataset revision,
termination and truncation, and simulator or hardware validity. Candidate
profiles may expose a compatible subset of measurements for comparison, while
the native prospective protocol and outcome record remain authoritative.

## Agentic finance

The finance program separates starting-policy qualification, candidate
measurement, training outcomes, material-control outcomes, and retrospective
cross-source analyses. A qualification profile may expose executable
completion, tool-protocol reliability, mixed-scenario coverage, control
violations, truncation, and realized cost under one pinned operations contract.
Candidate ranking begins only after the native decision rule has been frozen
and tested.

Infrastructure-invalid episodes remain distinct from failed finance work, and
control violations remain visible even when executable completion improves.
Published post-training comparisons can support the finance research program
without becoming pre-training coordinates for the prospective operations
study.

## Molecular design

The molecular program measures fixed replay banks under one RxnFlow starting
policy, synthesis environment, Q07820 oracle, and bank-size contract. Candidate
profiles expose the native construction-view summaries for target reward,
generated actions, oracle time, scaffold and ring-system coverage, route and
reaction coverage, and distance in the frozen graph representation. The
independent view qualifies the stability of the six-bank ordering and does not
contribute members or get averaged into those coordinates.

Molecule-specific eligibility, conformer, and pinned-oracle atom-type failures
remain explicit scientific zeroes in the native probe and are excluded from the
eligible bank pool. Every other parser, receptor, toolchain, docking-process,
and timeout failure remains an infrastructure failure. Profiles link the native
qualification records and preserve target-training outcomes as a separate
scientific object. A compatible six-bank panel therefore establishes
measurement conformance, not predictive or allocation evidence.

## Evolution

New domain functionality normally requires a new adapter or measurement
convention, not a new CLI command. New coordinates can be added by publishing a
definition and emitting them from the adapter. A changed estimator, unit, or
scientific meaning receives a new convention revision. The CLI rejects mixed
revisions in one candidate panel, allowing old and new studies to coexist.

The core schema changes only when the operational envelope itself changes, such
as adding a new integrity primitive. Such changes require an RFC, migration
notes, and conformance tests.

## Current integration boundary

The grouped policy-probe workflow has a complete native path from qualified
attempts to a content-addressed environment manifest. Cross-domain profiles
have a validated interchange contract, Python construction helpers, native CLI
conformance tests, and a real producer for the released Vero development
measurement. That adapter preserves a valid zero-signal result, positive model
cost, and the failed support gate as an unready profile.

The SylloGym release includes its complete outcome-blind component measurement
and frozen mixture weights. Its study-local adapter projects the three compared
mixtures without reading downstream outcomes. The PushT release still omits the
complete per-candidate pre-training vectors and remains an evidence release
rather than a profile producer. The LAB regression-risk record is a predictor
evaluation over tasks, not a candidate environment measurement, and remains a
separate scientific object.

The Business Bench finance adapter projects one completed Qwen3-8B
starting-policy qualification. It preserves zero completion, zero mixed-group
coverage, positive model cost, infrastructure failures, and the failed
readiness gates. Comparable candidate profiles remain unavailable until a
starting policy qualifies and the native candidate bank is measured.

The Vero formal-verification, molecular reaction-bank, SylloGym legal, Can
robotics, MLIP materials, and Business Bench finance studies now have native
profile producers at their respective evidence boundaries. Their profiles
establish record and measurement conformance. Predictive or allocation evidence
still requires the outcome protocol defined by each study. Additional producers
should first remain beside their experiment and satisfy the
[adapter conformance workflow](adapter-authoring.md).
