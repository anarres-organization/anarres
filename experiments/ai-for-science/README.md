# AI for Science

The AI for Science program studies how to make post-training for biological and
chemical research more predictable. It asks which environments help models
improve at scientific reasoning, tool use, experimental planning, and analysis
while preserving validity and relevance to the target research task.

Environment measurement in this domain must account for domain-specific rewards,
uncertainty, provenance, and experimental validity. These properties remain
explicit parts of the training and evaluation contract.
