# Health

Health environments differ in how much of the desired behavior an exact rule
can check, how much needs a documented lexical rule, and how much needs a
judge or an expert. This program starts with one question in mental-health
support:

> Which parts of language-model behavior in mental-health support conversations
> can be checked by an exact or documented rule, and how well do model judges
> agree with those checks?

All current work uses public literature and fictional synthetic tasks. Nothing
here is clinical advice or evidence of clinical validity.
